1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game application Neon Pop (also referred to as NEON POP: Bubble Odyssey, package identifier: com.aistudio.neonpop.kxmpzq), developed and published by the developer (contact: kunakom01@gmail.com), available on the Google Play Store.

Your privacy is important to us. This policy describes how data is handled when you download, install, and play Neon Pop, including our practices regarding third-party advertising services.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information Stored Locally On Your Device

Neon Pop operates primarily as a client-side game. The following data is generated and stored locally in your device's private app storage (Android SharedPreferences) and is never uploaded to our own external servers:

  • Game Progress & Stats: Unlocked Odyssey levels, high scores, stars, accuracy, shots fired, pop streaks, and total bubbles popped.
  • In-Game Economy: Neon coin balance and unlocked cosmetics (bubble trail skins, theme palettes).
  • Player Preferences: Audio volume settings (SFX & music), mute toggle, trajectory guide preference, colorblind symbols toggle, and reduced motion/flash accessibility options.
  • Ad Configuration Preferences: Diagnostic test mode settings and custom ad unit IDs entered in developer settings.

B. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party SDKs

When you play the game and interact with advertisements, third-party libraries (specifically Google Mobile Ads / AdMob and Google Play Services) may automatically collect certain device and technical information, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID), Android OS version, device model, hardware specifications, and screen resolution.
  • Network & General Location: IP address (used to infer general coarse geographic location such as country or city for localized ad delivery), carrier name, and connection type (Wi-Fi/Cellular).
  • Ad Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, video completions, reward verification callbacks, and ad performance metrics.
  • Diagnostics & Crash Logs: Performance data, application responsiveness, and error logs collected to maintain system stability and prevent fraud.

3. How Information Is Used

The information collected is used exclusively for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Core Gameplay Functionality: Storing your progress, highest unlocked levels, high scores, and audio preferences between game sessions.
  • Rewarding Ad Views: Delivering rewarded video ads when you explicitly opt in (e.g., opening Mystery Crates, claiming Extra Shots, or continuing a level) and validating the reward upon completion.
  • Ad Serving & Analytics: Allowing Google AdMob to deliver relevant ads, limit the frequency of the same ad, prevent click fraud, and monitor ad revenue.
  • App Performance & Stability: Identifying technical issues, resolving bugs, and ensuring compatibility across various Android device configurations.

4. Advertising & Google AdMob

Neon Pop integrates Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display rewarded video advertisements. We adhere strictly to the Google Play Developer Program Policies and AdMob Policy Guidelines:

  • User Choice & Opt-In: Rewarded ads are never shown without your explicit interaction. You must deliberately tap a button (such as "Watch Ad for Extra Shots" or "Mystery Crate") to initiate an ad.
  • Guaranteed Reward Delivery: When an ad finishes playing (or if the ad network has no inventory in test environments), you immediately receive your specified in-game bonus.
  • AdMob Advertising ID (AAID): Google AdMob utilizes the Google Advertising ID assigned to your Android device to serve personalized or contextual advertisements and to detect invalid traffic.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services by visiting Google's Partner Technologies Policy and Google Advertising Policies.

7. Data Storage, Security & Retention

  • No Centralized Account Database: Neon Pop does not maintain a remote database containing personal player profiles or identity records.
  • Local Storage: All game data is stored in the app's sandboxed local storage directory on your Android device. It is inaccessible to other non-privileged applications.
  • Retention: Local game save data remains on your device until you uninstall Neon Pop or clear app data via Settings > Apps > Neon Pop > Storage > Clear Data. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained in accordance with Google's published data retention schedules.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Neon Pop is designed as a family-friendly casual arcade puzzle game suitable for general audiences:

  • We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction) in violation of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) or General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
  • If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us without your consent, please contact us at kunakom01@gmail.com so we can take immediate corrective measures.
  • AdMob ad requests in our codebase are configured to adhere to Google Play's family-safe ad guidelines and avoid inappropriate advertising categories.

9. User Rights & Opting Out of Targeted Advertising

You have full control over advertising identifiers and tracking preferences on your Android device. You can adjust your preferences at any time:

How to Reset or Delete Your Advertising ID on Android:

  1. Open the Settings app on your Android device.
  2. Scroll down and tap Google (or Privacy > Google).
  3. Tap Ads (or Ad Privacy).
  4. Tap Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID). Once deleted, apps cannot use your Advertising ID to build interest-based profiles.

European Economic Area (EEA) & UK (GDPR) Rights:

If you reside in the EEA or UK, you have rights regarding access, rectification, portability, and erasure of personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation. Since we do not retain identifiable personal records on our own servers, you can exercise these rights directly on your device by clearing app storage, or through Google's privacy dashboard for ad-related data.

California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA) Notice:

We do not sell personal information as defined by California law. Third-party advertising partners may collect device identifiers as described in Section 2. You may exercise your right to opt out of interest-based ad sharing using the Android device settings described above.
